SECTION B — APPLIED QUESTIONS (70 MARKS)

Question 31 (14 marks)

A harare wholesaler faces a decision involving double-entry and error correction and receivables control and bank reconciliation. Managers have incomplete records, competing priorities and limited resources.

(a) Explain two relevant principles of double-entry and error correction and apply them to this organisation. (4 marks)

(b) Identify three pieces of information required to assess receivables control and bank reconciliation, and explain their importance. (6 marks)

(c) Recommend two justified management actions and explain how success would be measured. (4 marks)
