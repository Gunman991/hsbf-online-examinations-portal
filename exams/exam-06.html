SECTION A: Digital Marketing Strategy and Planning (20 Marks)
Scenario 1: The Struggling Clothing Boutique
UrbanStyle Boutique is a small clothing business operating in Harare. The business sells casual wear, shoes and accessories. It has a Facebook page with 2,500 followers and an Instagram account with 800 followers.
Despite having these followers, the business receives very few online orders. Its owner posts product photographs irregularly, does not identify a specific target audience and has never used paid advertising. Competitors are attracting more customers through TikTok videos, influencer partnerships and WhatsApp Business.
The owner has allocated a budget of $150 per month for digital marketing and wants to increase online sales within three months.
Questions
Identify and explain four weaknesses in UrbanStyle Boutique’s current digital marketing approach. (4 marks)
Develop a suitable target audience profile for the business, including demographic and behavioural characteristics. (4 marks)
Recommend four digital marketing channels or techniques the business should use within its budget. Justify each recommendation. (8 marks)
Propose two measurable objectives the business should achieve within three months. (4 marks)
SECTION B: Content Marketing, Social Media and Digital Branding (20 Marks)
Scenario 2: The Viral Post That Failed
FreshBite Zimbabwe sells healthy snacks and fresh juice. The company publishes a humorous TikTok video that receives 40,000 views and 2,000 likes. However, only five people place orders after viewing the video.
The marketing assistant celebrates the campaign as a major success because the video received thousands of views. The manager argues that the campaign failed because it generated almost no sales.
The company’s brand message is inconsistent: its TikTok account uses humorous language, its Facebook page uses formal language, and its product packaging presents the business as a premium health brand.
Questions
Explain the difference between reach, engagement and conversion, using evidence from the scenario. (6 marks)
Give three possible reasons why the viral video generated few orders. (3 marks)
Develop a content marketing plan containing four different types of content FreshBite could publish over one week. State the purpose of each type. (4 marks)
Explain three ways the company can maintain consistent digital branding across its platforms. (3 marks)
Recommend two methods of converting social media viewers into paying customers. (4 marks)
SECTION C: Search Engine Optimisation and Website Marketing (20 Marks)
Scenario 3: The Invisible Online Store
TechPoint Supplies sells laptops, printers and computer accessories. The business recently launched an online store but receives only 300 website visits per month.
The website has several problems:
Product descriptions contain very little useful information.
Product images load slowly.
The website is difficult to use on mobile phones.
Product pages lack clear headings and relevant search terms.
Customers cannot easily find delivery information or contact details.
The owner wants the website to appear more prominently in Google search results and increase online purchases without relying entirely on paid advertisements.
Questions
Define Search Engine Optimisation (SEO) and explain its importance to TechPoint Supplies. (3 marks)
Identify four website problems in the scenario and explain how each could affect traffic, user experience or sales. (4 marks)
Recommend four practical SEO improvements the business should implement. (8 marks)
Explain the difference between organic search results and paid search advertisements. (2 marks)
Recommend three key elements of an effective product landing page that could help increase purchases. (3 marks)
SECTION D: Paid Advertising, Budgeting and Performance Analytics (20 Marks)
Scenario 4: Choosing the Better Campaign
Glow Cosmetics runs two online advertising campaigns to promote a new skincare product. Both campaigns run for one week.
Performance measure | Campaign A | Campaign B
Advertising spend | $100 | $150
Impressions | 20,000 | 30,000
Clicks | 800 | 900
Purchases | 40 | 30
Revenue generated | $400 | $450
The marketing officer recommends Campaign B because it generated more revenue and impressions. The business owner wants to know which campaign used its advertising budget more efficiently.
Questions
Calculate the click-through rate (CTR) for Campaign A and Campaign B. (4 marks)
Calculate the conversion rate for each campaign using clicks as the denominator. (4 marks)
Calculate the cost per purchase for each campaign. (4 marks)
Calculate the return on ad spend (ROAS) for each campaign. (4 marks)
Recommend which campaign the business should prioritise, based on the calculations. Justify your answer using at least two performance measures. (4 marks)
Use the following formulas:
CTR = (Clicks ÷ Impressions) × 100
Conversion rate = (Purchases ÷ Clicks) × 100
Cost per purchase = Advertising spend ÷ Purchases
ROAS = Revenue generated ÷ Advertising spend
SECTION E: Integrated Digital Marketing Campaign and Professional Judgement (20 Marks)
Scenario 5: Launching an Online Learning Academy
SkillBridge Academy is a new training business offering online courses in digital marketing, graphic design and basic computer skills.
The academy has a limited marketing budget of $300 for its first month. It wants to attract at least 50 paid student registrations within 30 days.
The academy currently has no established social media following, no email subscriber list and no online advertising experience. Its website contains course descriptions, but there is no clear registration process. The owner is considering paying a popular influencer $250 to promote all the courses in one post.
Questions
Prepare a practical 30-day digital marketing campaign for SkillBridge Academy. Divide it into three phases and explain the main activities in each phase. (6 marks)
Allocate the $300 budget across at least three digital marketing activities. Justify your allocation. (4 marks)
Explain how the academy can use a landing page and a clear call to action to increase student registrations. (3 marks)
Identify three key performance indicators (KPIs) the academy should monitor and explain why each is relevant. (3 marks)
Evaluate whether spending $250 on a single influencer post is a sensible decision. Give two advantages, two risks and a reasoned recommendation. (4 marks)
