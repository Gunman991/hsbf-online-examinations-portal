Case Study: Delta Beverages and the Soft Drinks Market in Zimbabwe
Delta Beverages is one of Zimbabwe's leading beverage manufacturers, producing a wide range of products that cater to different consumer needs. The company operates in a highly competitive market where it faces competition from both imported soft drinks and local beverage brands. To maintain its leadership position, Delta has invested significantly in advertising campaigns, sales promotions, and an extensive distribution network that ensures its products are readily available across the country.
In recent years, the beverage industry has experienced notable changes in consumer preferences. Increasing health awareness among consumers has led to a growing demand for healthier beverage options with reduced sugar content. Market research conducted by the company revealed that many customers are becoming more conscious of the health risks associated with excessive sugar consumption, prompting a shift in purchasing behaviour.
In response to these market trends, Delta Beverages introduced a range of low-sugar products while continuing to strengthen its marketing efforts and product innovation strategies. However, the company still faces challenges such as rising production costs, changing economic conditions, and intense competition. Management is therefore exploring new marketing strategies to enhance customer satisfaction, increase sales, and sustain the company's competitive advantage in the Zimbabwean beverage market.
Question 1:
a. Explain four elements of the marketing mix applied by Delta Beverages. (10 Marks)
b. Discuss the factors influencing consumer buying behaviour in the beverage market. (10 Marks)
c. Evaluate the importance of market segmentation for Delta Beverages. (10 Marks)
d. Explain how marketing contributes to organisational success. (10 Marks)
