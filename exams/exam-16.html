SECTION A: APPLIED CASE STUDIES (100 MARKS)

QUESTION 1 — LAWFUL EVIDENCE ACQUISITION & CHAIN OF CUSTODY (25 MARKS)

Scenario: A Zimbabwean organisation is expanding operations into two provinces. Its management has limited funding, inconsistent procedures and rising customer complaints. Staff disagree about responsibilities, while stakeholders expect improved performance within six months.

(a) Identify and explain five specific issues or risks in this scenario that relate to lawful evidence acquisition. (5 marks)

(b) Analyse how weaknesses in chain of custody could affect the organisation and its stakeholders. (5 marks)

(c) Propose a practical five-step response plan addressing lawful evidence acquisition, with responsibilities and priorities. (5 marks)

(d) Explain how management should use evidence and stakeholder consultation to make defensible decisions about chain of custody. (5 marks)

(e) Recommend five measurable indicators or review methods to assess whether the proposed improvements are effective. (5 marks)
