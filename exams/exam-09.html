SECTION A: SHORT-ANSWER QUESTIONS [20 MARKS]
1. Define public relations and state two characteristics of effective PR. [4 marks]
2. Identify four publics that an organisation may need to communicate with. [4 marks]
3. State four objectives of public relations. [4 marks]
4. Distinguish between internal publics and external publics. [4 marks]
5. List four methods used by organisations to communicate with their publics. [4 marks]
SECTION B: STRUCTURED QUESTIONS [30 MARKS]
Answer any THREE questions. Each question carries 10 marks.
1. Explain five functions of public relations in an organisation. [10 marks]
2. Explain five factors that can influence an organisation's corporate image. [10 marks]
3. Describe five steps involved in planning a public relations campaign. [10 marks]
4. Explain five advantages of using social media as a public relations tool. [10 marks]
5. Explain five ways an organisation can manage communication during a crisis. [10 marks]
SECTION C: ESSAY QUESTIONS [50 MARKS]
Answer any TWO questions. Each question carries 25 marks.
1. “Effective public relations depends on maintaining mutually beneficial relationships between an organisation and its publics.” Discuss this statement. [25 marks]
2. Examine the role of media relations in promoting the image and reputation of an organisation. Use relevant examples. [25 marks]
3. A company has experienced a serious product-related crisis that has attracted negative attention on social media. As a public relations practitioner, discuss the strategies you would use to manage the crisis and restore stakeholder confidence. [25 marks]
4. Assess the importance of research, planning, communication and evaluation in successful public relations management. [25 marks]
