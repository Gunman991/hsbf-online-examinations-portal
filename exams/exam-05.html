CASE STUDY: EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW ABOUT VISITING VICTORIA FALLS.
Discover Africa (2025)
https://www.discoverafrica.com/blog/everything-you-need-to-know-about-visiting-victoria-falls/
Victoria Falls, known as "Mosi-oa-Tunya" (The Smoke That Thunders), is a premier UNESCO World Heritage site on the Zimbabwe-Zambia border, offering breath taking views of the world's largest curtain of falling water. It is a major hub for adventure tourism (bungee jumping, rafting) and wildlife safaris. Key risks include malaria, dangerous wildlife encounters, intense tourist overdevelopment, and climate-related fluctuations in water flow.
Key Facts about Victoria Falls
Location & Significance: Situated on the Zambezi River between Zimbabwe and Zambia, it is a UNESCO World Heritage site. The Zimbabwean side offers 75% of the viewing, while the Zambian side allows walking to the edge.
Size: It is 108 meters high and roughly 1,708 meters wide, forming the largest curtain of falling water in the world.
Best Time to Visit: The water flow peaks around March-April (wet season), while the dry season (August-December) offers better visibility of the rock face.
Activities: Famous for "adventure capital" activities including white-water rafting, bungee jumping from the Victoria Falls Bridge, helicopter flights, and sunset cruises.
Local Culture: The mist from the falls is culturally significant, with local beliefs associating it with ancestral spirits.
Victoria Falls is undoubtedly one of the most amazing natural wonders in the world. Every year, thousands of people come from all over the globe to see (and hear!) this magnificent sight for themselves. There are many reasons why visiting Victoria Falls is a great idea! For starters, the waterfalls are absolutely stunning and awe-inspiring to behold. Additionally, Victoria Falls is located in a beautiful and exotic location, making for an unforgettable trip. Plus, there are plenty of activities and attractions available in the area, ensuring that visitors will never be bored
The best time to visit Victoria Falls is between March and May, when water levels in the Zambezi River are at their highest and the Falls are at their most spectacular.
This is also the busiest time of year though, so if you’re looking to avoid the crowds, you might want to consider visiting during the shoulder season (November to February).
Some people prefer to visit Victoria Falls during the dry season, between June and October. This is when the water levels in the Zambezi River are at their lowest and you can get a closer view of the falls
Malaria is a serious concern in many parts of Africa, but luckily Victoria Falls is located in a low-risk area. However, it’s still important to take precautions against mosquito bites, such as using insect repellent and sleeping under a mosquito net, which most lodges will provide for you.
Victoria Falls tends to have hot summers from September through to April, with temperatures getting as high as 32 degrees Celsius (about 90 degrees Fahrenheit), and pleasantly mild winters from May through to August, with afternoon temperatures of around 25 degrees Celsius (77 degrees Fahrenheit).
