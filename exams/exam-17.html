SECTION A: COMPULSORY CASE STUDY (40 MARKS)
Case Study: Agricultural Supply Chain Challenges in Zimbabwe
A grain milling company based in Harare sources maize from farmers located in Mashonaland Central, Mashonaland West, and the Midlands provinces to meet the growing demand for its products. The company relies heavily on an efficient logistics system to transport maize from farming communities to its processing plant and warehouses. However, during the most recent harvesting season, the company encountered significant logistical challenges that disrupted its operations and affected overall performance.
The major challenges included delayed deliveries from suppliers, poor road infrastructure in some farming areas, recurring fuel shortages, and inadequate warehouse capacity. These challenges resulted in frequent transportation delays and increased distribution costs. Furthermore, insufficient storage facilities and poor warehouse conditions led to the deterioration of some maize stocks before processing, causing financial losses and reducing the quantity of marketable products available to customers.
As operational inefficiencies increased, the company began receiving customer complaints due to delayed order fulfilment and inconsistent product availability. Rising transportation and storage costs also put pressure on profitability. Concerned about the impact of these challenges on business performance and customer satisfaction, management has initiated a comprehensive review of its logistics operations with the aim of improving efficiency, reducing costs, strengthening inventory management, and enhancing service delivery to customers.
Question 1:
a. Identify and explain four logistics challenges faced by the company. (10 Marks)
b. Discuss the impact of poor inventory and warehouse management on the business. (10 Marks)
c. Recommend five strategies that the company can implement to improve its transportation system. (10 Marks)
d. Explain how effective logistics management can improve customer satisfaction and profitability. (10 Marks)
