Examination questions

Question 1

A business purchases equipment costing $18,000 by bank transfer. The bookkeeper incorrectly debits repairs expense and credits bank. Which correction is required?

Question 2

A business has opening capital of $75,000. During the year, the owner introduces an additional $12,000, makes drawings of $9,000, and the business earns a profit of $26,000. What is the closing capital?

Question 3

A business purchases goods on credit for $14,400, including sales tax at 20%. The business is registered for sales tax and can recover the input tax. Which entries correctly record the purchase?

Question 4

A trial balance shows debit balances exceeding credit balances by $3,600. Which journal entry would temporarily make the trial balance agree?

Question 5

The receivables control account has an opening debit balance of $22,500. Credit sales are $86,000, cash received from customers is $71,400, sales returns are $3,200, and irrecoverable debts written off are $1,100. What is the closing receivables balance?

Question 6

A supplier's account has an opening credit balance of $16,800. Credit purchases are $54,000, payments to the supplier are $42,500, and purchase returns are $2,700. What is the closing balance?

Question 7

A business's cash book shows a debit balance of $8,250 before bank reconciliation. The bank statement reveals bank charges of $150, a customer receipt of $1,200 not yet entered in the cash book, and an unpresented cheque of $900. What is the corrected cash book balance?

Question 8

A credit sale of $7,500 was correctly recorded in the sales account but was posted to the customer's receivables account as $5,700. What is the effect before correction?

Question 9

Goods are sold on credit for $24,000 plus sales tax of 15%. The customer subsequently returns goods with a net selling price of $4,000. What amount remains due from the customer, assuming no other transactions?

Question 10

A business purchases goods for $18,000 before sales tax at 15%. The supplier grants a trade discount of 10% before sales tax is calculated. What is the total invoice amount?

Question 11

A customer owes $12,000. The business offers a 5% settlement discount if payment is made promptly. The customer pays within the discount period. Assuming the receivable is stated before settlement discount, how much cash is received?

Question 12

A business makes taxable sales of $96,000 excluding sales tax at 15%. It makes taxable purchases of $52,000 excluding recoverable sales tax at the same rate. What sales tax is payable to the tax authority, before any other adjustments?

Question 13

At the year end, inventory has a cost of $48,000. It can be sold for $52,000, but completion costs of $3,000 and selling costs of $4,500 will be incurred. At what amount should the inventory be valued under IAS 2?

Question 14

Inventory records show:  1 March: 120 units at $8 each  8 March: 100 units at $9 each  15 March: 150 units issued  20 March: 80 units at $10 each  25 March: 70 units issued Using FIFO, what is the value of closing inventory?

Question 15

A business holds 200 units costing $12 each. It purchases a further 300 units at $15 each. It then sells 350 units. Using periodic weighted-average cost (AVCO), what is the value of closing inventory, rounded to the nearest dollar?

Question 16

A business has opening inventory of $35,000, purchases of $160,000, purchase returns of $8,000, carriage inwards of $5,000 and closing inventory of $42,000. What is cost of sales?

Question 17

A physical inventory count reveals inventory costing $6,400 that is damaged. It can be sold for $5,000 after incurring selling costs of $700. What adjustment is required to the inventory value?

Question 18

A machine costs $90,000, has an estimated residual value of $10,000 and a useful life of five years. It is purchased on 1 January. Using straight-line depreciation, what is the carrying amount after two full years?

Question 19

An asset costing $64,000 is depreciated at 25% per annum using the reducing-balance method. What is its carrying amount at the end of year two?

Question 20

A vehicle costing $48,000 was purchased on 1 April. The business depreciates vehicles at 20% per annum on cost and charges depreciation monthly, with no residual value. The financial year ends on 31 December. What depreciation expense should be recognised for the year of purchase?

Question 21

A company purchases a machine for $100,000. Installation costs are $6,000, staff training costs are $2,000, and initial testing costs necessary to make the machine operational are $3,000. What is the initial cost of the machine, excluding training costs?

Question 22

A machine has a carrying amount of $72,000 before a review of its useful life. The remaining useful life is revised from four years to three years, and the estimated residual value remains $6,000. Depreciation is charged on a straight-line basis. What is the revised annual depreciation charge from the date of the estimate change?

Question 23

An asset originally cost $80,000 and has accumulated depreciation of $52,000 at disposal. It is sold for $24,000. What is the profit or loss on disposal?

Question 24

A building has a carrying amount of $250,000 immediately before revaluation. It is revalued to $310,000, and there has been no previous revaluation decrease for that asset. What is the revaluation increase?

Question 25

Equipment is purchased on 1 January for $60,000 and depreciated at 20% per annum on cost. It is sold on 30 September of the following year. Depreciation is charged monthly, including the month of disposal. What is the carrying amount at disposal?

Question 26

A company spends $40,000 on research and $75,000 on development of a new product. The development expenditure meets all the recognition criteria for capitalisation under the applicable standard. What amount should be recognised as an intangible asset, assuming no other costs qualify?

Question 27

A recognised intangible asset costs $90,000 and has a useful life of six years with no residual value. It is available for use on 1 October, and the financial year ends on 31 December. What amortisation expense should be recognised for the year, assuming straight-line amortisation?

Question 28

Which statement about an intangible asset with an indefinite useful life is correct under IAS 38?

Question 29

A business pays rent of $36,000 on 1 October for the 12 months ending 30 September of the following year. The financial year ends on 31 December. What rent expense and prepayment should be reported at year end?

Question 30

Electricity expenses paid during the year total $18,400. The opening accrued electricity expense was $1,600 and the closing accrued electricity expense is $2,300. What electricity expense should be recognised in profit or loss?

Question 31

Insurance paid during the year totals $24,000. At the beginning of the year, prepaid insurance was $3,000. At the end of the year, prepaid insurance is $5,000. What insurance expense should be charged to profit or loss?

Question 32

A business receives $30,000 on 1 December for services to be provided evenly over the six months ending 31 May. Its financial year ends on 31 December. How much of the receipt should be recognised as revenue in the year ending 31 December?

Question 33

Trade receivables at year end are $80,000. A specific debt of $5,000 is identified as irrecoverable and must be written off. The business then wishes to maintain an allowance of 4% of the remaining receivables. What allowance is required?

Question 34

At the start of the year, the allowance for receivables was $2,400. At the end of the year, after writing off irrecoverable debts, the required allowance is $3,700. What is the effect of the allowance adjustment on profit or loss?

Question 35

A customer balance of $4,800 that was written off as irrecoverable in a previous period is unexpectedly recovered in cash. Which treatment is appropriate?

Question 36

A business has receivables of $125,000 before adjustments. It writes off $7,000 of irrecoverable debts and then recognises an allowance equal to 5% of the remaining receivables. What is the net receivables figure reported in the statement of financial position?

Question 37

A company faces a legal claim. At the reporting date, legal advisers conclude that an outflow of resources is probable and that the best estimate of the settlement is $45,000. The event giving rise to the obligation occurred before the reporting date. What is the appropriate accounting treatment under IAS 37?

Question 38

A company is defending a lawsuit. Its lawyers believe an outflow is possible but not probable. The potential amount is material. What is generally required under IAS 37?

Question 39

A manufacturer provides warranties on goods sold. Past experience indicates that 3% of units sold require minor repairs costing $20 each and 1% require major repairs costing $80 each. During the year, 10,000 units were sold. What warranty provision should be estimated using these probabilities?

Question 40

Which of the following is the strongest reason for not recognising a provision for future operating losses?

Question 41

A company issues 50,000 ordinary shares with a nominal value of $1 each for $1.40 per share. What amounts should be credited to share capital and share premium, respectively?

Question 42

A company has 200,000 ordinary shares of $0.50 each. It makes a 1-for-4 bonus issue. What is the number of shares in issue immediately after the bonus issue?

Question 43

A company has $400,000 of 8% loan notes outstanding for the whole year. Interest for the year has not yet been paid. What finance cost and year-end interest liability should be recognised?

Question 44

A company issues 100,000 $1 ordinary shares at $1.25 each. The issue proceeds are received in cash. Which entry correctly records the issue?

Question 45

A company has profit before finance costs of $150,000 and finance costs of $30,000. Income tax is $24,000. What is profit for the year, assuming no other items?

Question 46

A company has the following equity balances before a bonus issue:  Ordinary share capital: $300,000  Share premium: $80,000  Retained earnings: $120,000 The company makes a bonus issue of one new $1 share for every five existing $1 shares. The bonus issue is funded entirely from share premium. What are the balances of share capital and share premium immediately after the issue?

Question 47

A company has 12% loan notes of $250,000 outstanding from 1 January. On 1 July, it repays $50,000 of the loan notes. Interest is payable annually in arrears. What finance cost should be recognised for the year, assuming the repayment takes effect on 1 July?

Question 48

A company reports profit after tax of $84,000. During the year, it paid an ordinary dividend of $18,000 and transferred $10,000 from retained earnings to a general reserve. What is the net increase in total equity arising from these items, ignoring other movements?

Question 49

A company has 100,000 ordinary shares of $1 each in issue. It makes a rights issue of one new share for every four existing shares at $1.60 per share. All shareholders take up the offer and pay in cash. What is the total cash raised and the increase in share capital, respectively?

Question 50

A company has the following balances at the beginning of the year: Item Amount Ordinary share capital $250,000 Share premium $45,000 Retained earnings $70,000 During the year, the company makes a profit after tax of $62,000, pays dividends of $17,000, and issues ordinary shares with a nominal value of $30,000 for $1.50 per share. What is total equity at the end of the year, assuming no other changes?
