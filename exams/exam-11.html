A mining company currently operates separate systems for quality management, environmental management, and occupational health and safety. Employees complain about duplicated procedures, excessive paperwork, and conflicting responsibilities. Management is considering implementing an Integrated Management System (IMS)
State five benefits the company could gain from implementing an Integrated Management System.(25 marks)
A manufacturing organisation has implemented an Integrated Management System aligned with ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001. However, senior managers rarely attend management review meetings, provide limited resources, and leave IMS responsibilities entirely to the SHEQ department.
As a result, audit findings continue to increase, employee participation is low and system objectives are not being achieved.
a) Describe the role of top management in an effective Integrate Management System. (10marks)
b) Recommend actions top management should take to demonstrate leadership and commitment. (15marks).
