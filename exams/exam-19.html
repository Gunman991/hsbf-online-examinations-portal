SECTION A: ANSWER ALL QUESTIONS IN THIS SECTION
A non- governmental organisation implemented a six month clean water project in a rural community. The project involved drilling two boreholes, training community members on water management and establishing water point committees. At project completion, the Project Officer was tasked with preparing a project report to document the project’s performance and outcomes.
As the Project manager outline and explain five steps involved in preparing a project report. (25 marks)
a.) Explain the importance of analysing project information before writing the project report. (10 marks)
b) What is the primary purpose of project reports? (15 marks)
SECTION B: CHOOSE ANY TWO QUESTIONS TO ANSWER
Identify and explain any five types of project reports that may be written before, during or at the end of a project. (25marks)
A project Manager was assigned to prepare a report on a road construction project. The initial report contained inaccurate figures, poor organisation, grammatical errors and omitted important project challenges. Stakeholders found the report difficult to understand and requested a revised version.
Identify and explain five best practices that the project manager should follow when revising the report. (25marks)
What are some of the limitations of Project report writing and presentation? (25marks)
A local authority initiated a project to construct a Community Health Centre in a rural district. The project was scheduled to take 12 months and had a budget of USD 500,000. During implementation, several challenges occurred, including delayed material deliveries, increased construction costs and labour shortages. At the end of the project, the Project Manager was required to prepare a comprehensive project report for stakeholders, including donors, local authorities and community representatives. The report needed to communicate the project's objectives, activities undertaken, budget performance, achievements, challenges encountered, lessons learnt and recommendations for future projects.
Using the above case study, briefly explain the components of a project report. (25marks)
