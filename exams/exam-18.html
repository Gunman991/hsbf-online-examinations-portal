Differentiate between Monitoring and Evaluation. (5 Marks)
2. A municipality has implemented a waste management project aimed at improving environmental cleanliness within the city. The project includes waste collection, recycling initiatives, public awareness campaigns, and the construction of waste disposal sites. The municipality intends to use Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E) to assess the project's effectiveness and impact.
a) Explain the purpose of Monitoring and Evaluation of a project (25 marks)
SECTION B: Choose any two questions to answer
3. A non-governmental organisation (NGO) implemented a rural water supply project to improve access to safe drinking water in remote communities. The project involved drilling boreholes, constructing water storage tanks, and training community members on water management. One year after implementation, the donor agency requested an evaluation of the project's performance and sustainability.
a) Describe and explain three types of evaluation any project can conduct. (10marks)
b) Identify and explain five performance indicators that could be used to monitor the success of any project of your choice. (15 marks)
4. Chikoza (Pvt) Ltd is a company that produces plastic packaging for the food and beverage industry. To improve productivity, reduce operational costs and enhance product quality, the company invested in a new automated production line. The project included the purchase of machinery, employee training, installation of monitoring systems and process redesign.
Six months after implementation, the company experienced production delays, employee resistance to the new technology, increased machine downtime and customer complaints regarding product defects. A review revealed that key stakeholders, including machine operators, supervisors, customers, suppliers and maintenance personnel were not adequately involved in the monitoring and evaluation (M&E) process.
a) Discuss the importance of stakeholder participation in Monitoring and Evaluation of the automation project. (15 Marks)
b) Discuss challenges that may arise during monitoring and evaluation and propose appropriate mitigation measures. (10marks)
6. Discuss how a logical framework (Log frame) can support project Monitoring and Evaluation. (25marks)
